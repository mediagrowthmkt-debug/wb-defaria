What a general contractor does

One contractor from the first walkthrough to the final inspection.

Most remodels go wrong in the gaps between trades: the plumber waits on the framer, the tile goes in before the inspection, the cabinets arrive before the floor is level. As your general contractor, DeFaria plans the full scope before anything is torn out, pulls the building and trade permits, schedules framing, plumbing, electrical, insulation, drywall, tile, finish carpentry and paint in the right order, orders materials early and keeps one person accountable for the schedule and the budget.

The owner is on the job. You get a written, itemized estimate, a clear start date, updates while the work is happening and a final walkthrough before the last payment. That is the difference between hiring a crew and hiring a contractor who manages the whole project.

FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before hiring a general contractor in Lynn, MA

What does a general contractor in Lynn, MA do?

A general contractor plans the project, pulls the building permits, schedules every trade (framing, plumbing, electrical, tile, finish carpentry and paint), orders materials and is the single point of contact until the final inspection. At DeFaria, the owner walks the project with you and stays on it from estimate to cleanup.

Which towns does DeFaria Construction serve?

DeFaria is based at 24 Fiske Ave in Lynn and works across Essex County and Middlesex County, from Lynn, Swampscott, Salem, Peabody and Beverly to Cape Ann, the Merrimack Valley and towns like Carlisle, Acton, Bedford and Tewksbury.

How much does a remodel cost on the North Shore in 2026?

Real 2026 ranges from our projects: a kitchen refresh runs about $18,000 to $30,000, a mid-range kitchen $35,000 to $65,000 and a full gut $70,000 to $120,000 and up. Bathrooms run about $10,000 to $20,000 for a refresh and $20,000 to $45,000 for a full remodel. Basements run about $35 to $90 per square foot.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. DeFaria pulls the building permit and the trade permits the town requires, schedules the inspections and keeps the paperwork with the project, so you never have to deal with the building department on your own.
