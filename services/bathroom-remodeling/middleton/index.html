Waterproofing and ventilation

Waterproofing and ventilation built for Middleton winters

In Essex County the temperature swing between a hot shower and a cold exterior wall drives condensation, so we build every Middleton bathroom to shed water and dry out fast. Waterproof membranes, sloped shower pans and sealed seams keep water where it belongs.
