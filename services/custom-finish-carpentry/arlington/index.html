Process

How a Arlington built-in or trim project runs, step by step

Every Arlington finish carpentry job is drawn and priced before any wood is cut.

  1. Walkthrough, measurements and a conversation about style and use.
  2. Drawing or clear sketch with dimensions, then a fixed estimate.
  3. Material selection: paint-grade or stain-grade, hardware, finish.
  4. Shop work: boxes, face frames, doors and drawers built and fitted.
  5. Installation, scribed to walls and floors.
  6. Caulk, fill, prime and paint or stain.
  7. Final walkthrough and hardware adjustments.

When a Arlington built-in needs a new outlet, a moved switch or a wall opened, DeFaria coordinates the permit with the Town of Arlington Inspectional Services before installation.
