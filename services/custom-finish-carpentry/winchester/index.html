Process

How a Winchester built-in or trim project runs, step by step

Every Winchester finish carpentry job is drawn and priced before any wood is cut.

  1. Site visit and measurements of walls, floor and ceiling.
  2. Design with dimensions, outlets and lighting marked.
  3. Wood, finish and hardware chosen with you.
  4. Pieces built and dry-fitted in the shop.
  5. Install day: set, level, scribe and fasten into framing.
  6. Trim, fill, sand and finish.
  7. Walkthrough and adjustments before we leave.

Value

Built-ins or furniture: which makes more sense?

Freestanding furniture is cheaper up front and moves with you. Built-ins cost more but use every inch of an awkward wall, look original to the house and stay with the home when you sell.

They make the most sense in alcoves, around fireplaces and windows, under stairs and in rooms where a standard bookcase leaves gaps. For a quick fix on a regular wall, furniture can be the better buy, and we will say so.
